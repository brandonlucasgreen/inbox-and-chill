Brand kit

Inbox & Chill

Everything waiting on you, in one queue — and a way to empty it.

Position

Quiet, warm, and honest about being small

The brand should feel like the app: keyboard-first, unhurried, and clearly the work of one person rather than a company. Every value below is inherited from something that already exists across bgreen.lol, Unstream, or Social Sindy — nothing here was invented in isolation.

The mark

A peace sign, not a tray

Microsoft's Fluent Emoji victory hand, Flat variant, MIT licensed — the actual emoji rather than a logo of it. The tray is what the app does; the peace sign is what it's for. Every competitor in this space signals volume and urgency. This one signals you're allowed to be done.

Size ramp

Rendered at true pixel sizes. The Flat variant holds its shape to 16px; the Color variant's skin gradient turns pink on plum and muddies below 32.

Construction

1024 × 1024 canvas, rounded square 824 × 824 inset at (100, 100) with a 185.4 corner radius — Apple's macOS icon grid. The emoji sits in a 640 × 640 box centred on the canvas: translate(192,192) scale(20) from its native 32 × 32 viewBox.

Do

  • Use the Flat variant, not Color or 3D.
  • Ship the MIT licence file alongside it in any distribution.
  • Leave clear space of 10% of the icon width, already built into the 824-in-1024 inset.
  • Use the wordmark instead of the mark below 16px.

Don't

  • Recolor the hand. It ships in Fluent's own #FFC83D / #D67D00.
  • Rotate, skew, or re-draw it. If it needs to change, change the ground.
  • Crop the artwork out of its square.
  • “Correct” the emoji's yellow to match --warm. They're neighbours, not twins.

Menu bar glyph

Two states, one alpha mask

Both glyphs are template images — a single alpha mask that macOS tints for the light or dark bar. Hollow means the queue is empty; solid means something is waiting.

12 light bar
12 dark bar

The hollow state is Fluent's High Contrast variant used as-is — already one color, so its alpha is the line art. The solid state is the Flat variant with every fill collapsed to one color, which unions its detail strokes into a silhouette. A multi-color emoji can never be a menu bar glyph directly: a template image carries no color of its own, only coverage.

Palette

Plum ground, cream text, one warm note

The plum descends from bgreen.lol's accent; the amber is the same warm note Social Sindy's mark carries against its cool chrome; the cream is Unstream's light ground. The icon carries Fluent's own yellow rather than --warm — neighbours, not twins. If two things on a screen are amber, one of them is wrong. It is not a body-text color, not a link color, and not a background wash.

Typography

SN Pro, with mono for anything mechanical

SN Pro is the face across bgreen.lol and Social Sindy, loaded from Google Fonts with a system-ui fallback. This page renders in the fallback, so treat the specimen below as scale and rhythm rather than letterforms.

Display Get to zero
Body Items flow in, you act on them, and the queue goes to zero. clamp(1rem, 0.96rem + 0.22vi, 1.125rem) at 1.5–1.6 line-height.
Utility Eyebrows, labels, tokens
Keys Shortcuts are a core feature, so set them properly: ⌘P E S ⌘Z — never plain text.
Case Sentence case in prose and headings. The lowercase nav on bgreen.lol is a site mannerism, not a brand rule.

None of this applies inside the macOS app — use stock SwiftUI chrome there. The one rule that carries over: focus and selection are neutral, not system blue. A tonal Color.primary step over hover plus a selection-only hairline. System blue reads as an alert rather than a cursor.

Voice

Write it the way you'd write a post

If a line would look out of place on bgreen.lol, it's wrong for the landing page.

Do

  • First person. “I built this because…”, not “Inbox & Chill was built…”
  • Lead with the concrete thing it does, not the feeling it gives you.
  • Use real numbers. “1,199 GitHub notifications” beats “a lot of noise.”
  • Name the tradeoff out loud. “This is pre-release. Expect rough edges.”
  • Say what it isn't — triage queue, not a client. That distinction is the product.

Don't

  • Hype verbs: supercharge, revolutionize, unlock, 10x, effortlessly.
  • Productivity moralizing. The app doesn't think the reader is lazy.
  • Fake scale. One person made this; don't write “we.”
  • Urgency or FOMO. It's an app about not being urgent.
  • Feature lists where a sentence would do.

Naming

Inbox & Chill, inchill

  • The app is Inbox & Chill — ampersand, both words capitalized, no hyphen.
  • The CLI is inchill, lowercase, always in mono.
  • Bundle ID lol.bgreen.inboxandchill.
  • Never abbreviate to “I&C.”
